Hi [Name],

The lending platform is now working end to end in our cloud sandbox and ready for a demo. In short, here is what we have built and tested:

- Loan lifecycle: product setup, loan creation, disbursement, EMI, part-payment and foreclosure, with schedules, interest accruals and customer statements.
- Collateral and limits: a separate service holds facilities and collateral. Secured loans cannot be disbursed unless their collateral and facility are valid. Facility and collateral changes need a second person to approve.
- Accounting: an internal ledger posts the accounting entries for each loan event, with a daily reconciliation.
- End of day: batch jobs run in order and advance the business date. A failed run can now be rerun, and jobs already completed for a date are skipped.
- Admin console: one console with separate logins for batch, loan and limits operations, plus inquiry screens (accounts, overdue, payments, ledger and batch status).
- Automated proof: an end-to-end test console runs secured and unsecured loan flows through the whole platform and produces a report.
- Reset between demos: one Maintenance screen clears the loans, collateral and batch history and resets the business date, so each demo starts clean.

Two things to know before the demo:
- The platform runs on free-tier cloud hosting, so each service takes about a minute to wake up. I'll start them 20 minutes before we begin.
- The link to an external core-banking ledger is still a placeholder, so the ledger outbox screen shows every entry as failed. This is expected and doesn't affect balances or end of day. The real postings are on the accounting screens.

The customer portal is designed (wireframes and technical specification) but not built yet. It is the next step after we finish more life-cycle testing.
