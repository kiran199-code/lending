# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- **Accounting engine, Phase A (event-driven double-entry journal).** Built from *Accounting Entries Requirements v0.1* (157 requirements, 21 scenarios). Loan events now write balanced journals in the same database transaction as the business change; what is debited and credited is configuration (event templates, rules, roles and routes), not code. Later phases (NPA suspense, provisioning and write-off, waiver and reversal, multi-currency and revaluation, delivery to an external ledger) are not built; the tables and events they need are already seeded.
  - **Schema.** Migrations **V16** (core: event, tag, role, ledger, GL account, route, template and rule tables; partitioned `acct_journal` / `acct_journal_line` by business date, 2026-07 to 2027-12 and kept ahead by partition maintenance; idempotency, reversal link, rounding carry, posting exception, daily balance, reconciliation, period and configuration audit tables) and **V17** (seed: 31 events of which 12 are active, 73 amount tags, 26 roles, 3 ledgers, 26 GL accounts, 26 routes, 12 approved templates, 51 rules). Journals are append-only: a trigger rejects UPDATE and DELETE.
  - **Events journalled today:** `DISBURSE`, `INT_ACCRUE`, `INT_TRUEUP`, `DEMAND_RAISE`, `ADVANCE_ADJUST`, `PENAL_ACCRUE`, `EMI_PAYMENT`, `PART_PAYMENT`, `FORECLOSURE`, plus `OPENING_BALANCE` for cutover. Hooks are one call each in `LoanAccountService`, `InterestAccrualService`, `DemandService`, `PenalInterestService` and `EmiPaymentService`; the posting runs inside the caller's transaction, so a journal that cannot be written rolls the payment back. Interest and penal accrual are the exception: they are queued as posting exceptions so the loan keeps moving, and an exception that gates end of day holds the night until it is retried or waived.
  - **Interest is trued up at demand.** The journal accrues the rounded running total (a carry per account keeps it within a paisa of the 4-decimal accrual table). When a demand is raised, the receivable is topped up or down to exactly the interest demanded, so a missed day, an accrual past the due date or a paisa of rounding cannot leave a residue. Foreclosure does the same before it closes the receivable.
  - **Idempotent.** Every posting has a key (`event|sourceType|sourceId|qualifier`); posting twice returns the first journal. Replaying a payment, a demand run or an accrual changes nothing.
  - **Modes.** `accounting.enabled` is the master switch. Each event can be OFF, SHADOW (journals are stored and shown but not counted in balances) or LIVE. Templates and routes are maker-checked (`X-Actor`; the approver must differ from the author), versioned (approving a new version ends the previous one the day before) and every change is written to `acct_config_audit`.
  - **APIs** (`/api/v1/accounting`): journals (search and one with lines), trial balance, events, ledgers, GL accounts, routes, templates (draft, update, validate, submit, approve, reject, retire), `PUT /events/{code}`, a simulator that shows the journal an event would produce without writing it, posting exceptions (list, gate, retry, waive), `POST /snapshot`, `POST /reconciliation/run`, `GET /reconciliation`, `POST /opening-balance`, and `GET /api/v1/loan-accounts/{id}/journals`.
  - **Reconciliation.** Nine checks: every ledger balances (per branch), no exception holds up end of day, every journal carries its account's currency and branch (`RECON_ACCOUNT_DIMENSIONS`), and for principal not due, principal due, interest accrued (tolerance `accounting.recon-interest-tolerance`, default 0.01), interest due, penal interest and advance, the ledger equals the loan tables **account by account**. It compares with the sub-ledger as it stands now, so it runs for the current business date only (an earlier date is refused). With accounting off it reports a pass with nothing compared.
  - **Currency and branch (migration V18).** Two reference tables, `currency` (seeded INR) and `branch` (seeded ONLINE), and a required `currency_code` / `branch_code` on every loan account (defaults INR / ONLINE, so existing accounts and journals take the defaults and need no data fix). They are chosen at account creation (`currencyCode`, `branchCode` on create-loan and bulk create; both optional) and **fixed for the life of the account**. Every journal for an account, including the opening-balance job, is posted in the account's currency at the account's branch; the journal header and each line store the branch, and daily balances, the trial balance, the balance snapshot and the reconciliation are kept per branch. Routes take an optional `branch_code`: a route that names a branch outranks one that does not (specificity branch 8 > product 4 > stage 2 > currency 1), and a route without a branch serves every branch, so adding a branch needs no route unless its GL accounts differ. `GET/POST /api/v1/reference/currencies`, `PUT .../currencies/{code}/active`, `GET/POST /api/v1/reference/branches`, `PUT .../branches/{code}` add more. **Place-holders, not multi-currency yet:** a new currency is created inactive, and activating any currency other than the base (`accounting.base-currency`, INR) is refused with 409 unless `accounting.multi-currency-enabled=true`; even then the engine refuses mixed-currency journals until Phase E (FX). The base currency and the default branch (`lending.default-branch`, ONLINE) cannot be deactivated. Admin console: Currencies and Branches lists (read-only), branch/currency selectors on Create loan, a Branch filter and column on Journals and Trial balance, and currency/branch on Account detail and the accounts list.
  - **Cutover.** `POST /api/v1/accounting/opening-balance` journals every existing account's position as an `OPENING_BALANCE` (accounts that already have any journal are skipped, each account is its own transaction, and the rounding carries are seeded so the next accrual day continues correctly). Run it once, after V16/V17 are applied and before relying on the journal for accounts opened earlier.
  - **Batch jobs** (batch migration **V7**): `ACCOUNTING_RECONCILIATION_DAILY` (priority 140) runs the reconciliation and **fails end of day with the failed checks named**, so the business date never moves over a break; `ACCOUNTING_BALANCE_SNAPSHOT_DAILY` (priority 150) writes the day's balance of every GL account. Both run after every job that can post a journal.
  - **Admin console:** new **Accounting** menu: Journals (filter, open a journal to see its lines), Trial balance, Posting exceptions (retry, waive with a reason) and Reconciliation (latest result, Run now). Admin API under `/api/admin/accounting`. Maintenance clear-data now also empties the accounting tables, and the e2e cleanup script purges them under the same opt-in the append-only trigger requires (`acct.allow_purge`).
  - **Not built in Phase A (limits to know):** template and route maker-checker has no screen yet (use the API); an accrual that fails and is retried after a later demand has already trued up the same interest can be counted twice, which the reconciliation reports for that account; corrections are by new journals only (no reversal API until Phase D); no delivery to an external ledger (Phase F); one base currency (INR) is journalled, the multi-currency columns are present and unused.
  - **Deploy order:** lending-service first (it runs V16, V17 and V18), then run `POST /api/v1/accounting/opening-balance` for existing data, then lending-batch-service (V7 adds the two jobs; without the opening balance the first reconciliation will fail for every pre-existing account), then lending-admin-service.
  - **Tests:** `ExprTest`, `RoundingCarryTest`, `RouteResolverTest` (including branch specificity), `ReferenceDataServiceTest`, `JournalAssemblerTest`, `TemplateSelectorTest`, `AccountingFlowTest` (the flows end to end against in-memory repositories), `AccountingIntegrationTest` (against Postgres: disbursement, accrual, demand, payment, late payment with penal interest, foreclosure, idempotency, reconciliation), `FlywayMigrationTest` (tables, seed, append-only trigger), `LendingBackedJobsTest` and `LendingServiceClientTest` (the two jobs), `AccountingControllerTest` (admin), `TeardownSqlTest`.
- **Penal interest (Phase 2 of the penal-interest requirement).** Overdue instalments now earn penal interest, charged by the daily job, shown in every payable figure and paid first by the waterfall. Defaults are the requirement document's placeholders: **24% p.a., the account's day-count (ACT/365), 0 grace days, no cap, switched on**; all four are set per product.
  - **Product settings (Products screen).** New columns on `loan_product` (migration **V15**): `penal_interest_enabled`, `penal_interest_rate` (0 to under 100), `penal_grace_days` (0 to 365) and `penal_cap_percent` (blank = no cap, otherwise above 0 and up to 1000, as a % of the demand's principal + interest). The Products screen has a *Penal interest* column and edit-dialog fields (On/Off, rate, grace days, cap %). Settings are read when the charge is made, so a change applies to **every account of the product from the next business date**; each daily row keeps the rate it was charged at. Existing products get the defaults. Product API: the four fields are optional on create/update; if `penalInterestEnabled` is sent all four are applied together (a missing rate means 24, grace 0, cap none); if it is left out the product's penal settings are untouched.
  - **Charge.** For each day later than due date + grace days, on a demand with principal or interest unpaid: unpaid principal + interest at the close of that day (payments dated that day or earlier excluded) x rate / days in year (account day-count, 4 decimals). Simple, not compounded; stops at the cap; one row per demand per day (`penal_interest`, unique on demand + date, so a rerun changes nothing and a job that missed days catches up on the base each day would have had). Runs for ACTIVE and NPA_* accounts; stops for CLOSED, FORECLOSED and WRITTEN_OFF; rows are flagged `suspended` from SUBSTANDARD onward. Running totals sit on `loan_demand` (`penal_accrued` to 4 decimals, `penal_paid` to 2).
  - **Daily job.** `PENAL_INTEREST_CALCULATION_DAILY` (priority 20, after interest accrual) now has a body: per account it calls `POST /api/v1/loan-accounts/{id}/penal-interest?businessDate=`. `GET .../penal-interest` lists the daily charges. Eleven of the 37 job slots now call lending-service.
  - **Waterfall.** Penal interest is paid first, oldest demand first, then each demand's interest and principal as before. A demand is CLEARED only when principal, interest **and** penal interest are paid; the schedule row goes PAID as soon as principal + interest are paid. Penal interest does not itself earn penal interest.
  - **Payments do not depend on the job.** A payment accrues penal interest through the day before its value date before it is applied, so the amount asked for is the same whether or not the job has run (an instalment due 5 Nov and paid 14 Nov owes 8 days: 6th to 13th). Foreclosure adds open penal interest to the payoff. `amount-payable` and `payments/preview` include unposted days (read only) so the figure shown is the figure posted.
  - **Screens and records.** `emi_transaction.penal_paid` is stored and returned as `penalInterestPaid`. Pay instalment, Account detail, Payments, Foreclosure and Part-payment show a penal interest column/row; the statement PDF has a *Towards penal interest* column; the ledger payloads carry the penal amount; Maintenance lists `penal_interest`.
  - **Not built (limits to know):** per-account override of rate or grace days, waiver of penal interest, and recomputation when a payment is back-dated or reversed (days already charged stay as charged). Penal terms of a product apply to all its existing accounts, not only new ones.
  - **Deploy order:** lending-service first (it runs V15), then lending-batch-service, then lending-admin-service (it reads the new columns directly). Tests: `PenalInterestCalculatorTest`, `PenalInterestIntegrationTest`, `LoanProductServiceTest`, `LoanProductValidationTest`, `LendingBackedJobsTest`, `MaintenanceServiceTest`, `FlywayMigrationTest` updated. **Not built or run here** (Maven Central is not reachable); the calculator was checked in isolation with plain javac and the admin frontend with `tsc`. Run `mvn test` on all modules, then set a product's penal settings, run the end of day on a missed instalment for a few days and open Pay instalment.
- **Demands, payment waterfall and payments on overdue accounts (Phase 1 of the penal-interest requirement).** Built from the requirement document *Penal interest, demand and payment appropriation*; penal interest itself came in Phase 2 (see the entry above).
  - **Demand on the due date.** New tables `loan_demand` (one row per account and instalment: principal and interest due and paid, status OPEN / PART_PAID / CLEARED) and `payment_allocation` (one line per penal interest / interest / principal / advance bucket a payment touched), plus `loan_account.days_past_due` and `loan_account.advance_balance` (migration **V14**). New batch job **`DEMAND_RAISE_DAILY`** (batch migration **V6**, priority 5, first in the daily chain) calls `POST /api/v1/loan-accounts/{id}/demands?businessDate=` for every ACTIVE and NPA account. Raising is idempotent and catches up missed days; a payment also raises any due demand first, so payments do not depend on the batch having run.
  - **Waterfall.** A payment clears the oldest demand first, interest before principal (penal interest, once built, comes before both). Any amount above zero is accepted. Money beyond everything due is **held as an advance** (EMI payment) and applied when the next demand is raised, or goes to principal (part-payment). Plain Java class `PaymentWaterfall` shared by posting and preview (`PaymentPlanner`).
  - **Payments on NPA accounts.** EMI, part-payment and foreclosure now accept ACTIVE and every NPA_* status (before, only ACTIVE could pay, so an account that fell behind could never be brought current). After each payment the status and `days_past_due` are recalculated, and the account closes when every instalment is paid.
  - **Read APIs.** `GET /api/v1/loan-accounts/{id}/amount-payable?asOf=` (open demands oldest first, overdue, due today, advance, next instalment) and `POST /api/v1/loan-accounts/{id}/payments/preview` (how a payment would be applied, changes nothing). Payment responses now carry `allocations` and `advanceBalance`. Admin forwards both: `GET /api/admin/operations/accounts/{id}/amount-payable`, `POST .../payments/preview`.
  - **Pay instalment screen** shows the amount payable (overdue, due today, advance, total) with the open demands, pre-fills the total, lets the user pay any amount, shows how it will be applied before confirming, and shows the allocation on the receipt. NPA accounts can now be paid from it.
  - **Cutover for existing accounts.** The first `raiseDue` for an account creates demands for every due instalment of its current schedule; instalments already PAID become CLEARED demands. `days_past_due` is stored by the classification run. Rate reset and part-payment only re-price or regenerate instalments that have no demand yet.
  - **Changed behaviour to be aware of:** the EMI amount no longer has to equal the instalment exactly; a payment made before the due date is held as an advance and applied on the due date (before, it paid the next instalment on the spot); a part-payment first clears whatever is due. The e2e suite's expectations of the payment response should be checked.
  - Tests: `PaymentWaterfallTest`, `PaymentPlannerTest`, `DemandServiceIntegrationTest`, `LendingBackedJobsTest` (demand job), `AccountOperationsControllerTest` (amount payable and preview); `FlywayMigrationTest`, `MaintenanceServiceTest`, `AccountLevelBatchOperationsTest` updated. **Not built or run here** (Maven Central is not reachable from the working environment): run `mvn test` on all modules, redeploy lending-service, lending-batch-service and lending-admin-service, then test a missed instalment on one account.
- **Admin console: Account operations and a sectioned menu.** New screens for **Create loan, Disburse loan, Pay instalment, Part-payment, Foreclosure** and **Bulk create / disburse** (`#/ops/create`, `/ops/disburse`, `/ops/instalment`, `/ops/part-payment`, `/ops/foreclosure`, `/ops/bulk`). Each looks the account up, shows its figures, asks for confirmation in a pop-up that names the business date, and shows the result or lending-service's own error message. Instalment, part-payment and foreclosure carry an `Idempotency-Key` (the same form values reuse the same key), foreclosure needs `FORECLOSE` typed, and the bulk screen takes up to 200 pasted lines and reports every line. The admin service forwards the calls to lending-service (`POST /api/admin/operations/...` -> `/api/v1/loan-accounts/...`), so it still never writes to the database itself; it needs `LENDING_SERVICE_URL`. The left menu is now in folding sections: Dashboard, **Product**, **Account**, **Inquiry** (Accounts, Upcoming payments, Overdue, Payments, Interest accruals, Ledger outbox), **Batch** (Batch jobs, End of day) and **Administration** (Configuration, Maintenance). New classes `LendingAccountClient`, `AccountOperationsController`; test `AccountOperationsControllerTest`. Rebuild and redeploy `lending-admin-service` only.
- **E2E console: start date field.** The hosted console (`lending-e2e-test`) now has a Start date box next to accounts and max simulated days, sent as `start-date`. Before, the
  console could not change it and the built-in default (2026-11-01) was refused once the platform business date had moved past it. It must be the current business date or later.
- **End of day screen in the admin console** (`/#/eod`): shows the business date and the next one before you start or restart, asks for confirmation, and follows the run
  with a per-job progress table that refreshes itself (3 s while running, 10 s otherwise). Restart resumes a stopped run (finished jobs are skipped). The start call carries
  the date shown on screen and is refused with 409 if the business date has moved on. Admin API: `GET /api/admin/eod/status`, `POST /api/admin/eod/run`.
  Batch service: `GET /api/v1/batch-admin/eod/status` now includes live `progress` (new `EodListener`, `EodProgress`; `EndOfDayService.run(actor, listener)`).
  Tests: `EodProgressTest`, `EodControllerTest`. Needs `BATCH_SERVICE_URL` and `BATCH_ADMIN_PASSWORD` on the admin service.

### Fixed
- **No payment could be saved: every EMI, part-payment and foreclosure returned HTTP 500.** The payment record (`emi_transaction`, a partitioned table keyed by `transaction_id` + `value_date`) was mapped with a generated id on one half of a composite key, which Hibernate refuses (`JpaSystemException: Identity generation isn't supported for composite ids`, seen at `EmiPaymentService.foreclose`). The whole request is one transaction, so nothing was half-written (account 17 stayed ACTIVE), but no payment ever posted - the likely reason for *no payments triggered* in run E2E-261013153527-691 (to be confirmed by the next run). `transaction_id` is now taken from the table's own sequence before the save (`EmiTransactionRepository.nextTransactionId()`, used by `EmiPaymentService.saveNew` in all three payment paths) and `@GeneratedValue` is gone from the entity. Tests: new `EmiTransactionRepositoryIntegrationTest` (saves and reads back an EMI, a part-payment and a foreclosure record); `LoanAccountRepositoryIntegrationTest` now assigns the id too. Rebuild and redeploy `lending-service` only. Not yet built or run against a database here - post one payment (the Pay instalment screen will do) after the redeploy to confirm.
- **lending-service log was flooded with SQL.** `logging.level.org.hibernate.SQL` is now `info` (it was `debug`, which printed every statement, formatted); set it back to `debug` to see statements again.
- **End of day screen sat on "Loading..." while lending-service was asleep.** The batch status call read the business date from lending-service with retries, so a 502 from a
  cold-starting lending-service held the page for a minute or more. The status call now never waits: the date is read in the background and shown once it arrives (the
  screen says so and keeps checking), and the admin service gives the batch service only 20 s for a status check. The "start" call now asks the batch service to compare the
  date on screen with lending-service's own date (`POST /eod/run?expectedBusinessDate=`), refusing with 409 if it moved. Test `EndOfDayCoordinatorTest`.
- **Tasklet jobs with a timeout failed with "No business date in the job context"** (seen on `GL_POSTING_RECONCILIATION_DAILY`, which stopped end of day).
  `TimeoutTaskletDecorator` runs the tasklet on a separate guard thread, but the business date and job params are thread-local and were set on the
  step's thread. The context is now handed over to the guard thread (`BatchJobContext.snapshot()/restore()`). Affects every tasklet job that has
  `timeout_minutes` set. Test `TimeoutTaskletDecoratorTest`.
- **End of day failed outright when lending-service answered 502** (free-tier cold start or redeploy). The business-date calls (read, start end of day,
  advance) retried for only ~3 s. They now retry up to 9 times with a doubling pause capped at 30 s (about 1.5-2 minutes), still only on 429/502/503/504
  and connection errors; per-account calls keep the short retry so a down service does not stall every account.
- **End of day stopped after the first job it launched** (each run completed one job, the next run the next). The batch job launcher is asynchronous, so
  `SpringBatchEodJobGateway` judged the job while it was still STARTING/STARTED and reported it as failed. It now waits until the job reaches a final
  state (`batch.eod.job-timeout-minutes`, default 180; `batch.eod.poll-interval-ms`) and only then starts the next job in priority order, so one run
  works through all the daily jobs and advances the business date. A job that fails, or is still running after the timeout, stops the chain and the
  reason is logged. Test `SpringBatchEodJobGatewayTest`.
- **Interest accrual (and other partitioned jobs) could silently skip accounts.** Same root cause as the `ReaderNotOpenException`: when partitions
  overwrote each other's reader, one partition could read another's id range and its own range was never processed, with no error (the job still
  ended COMPLETED). Fixed by the per-thread reader (below); each partition now also logs its range and counts so a gap is visible.
- **Batch partitioned jobs failed with `ReaderNotOpenException: Reader must be open before it can be read`** (seen on `DPD_UPDATE_DAILY`, which left the
  end-of-day run FAILED). All partitions of a job share one `PartitionRangeItemReader`, and it kept its per-partition delegate reader in a plain field,
  so partitions starting at the same time overwrote each other's reader before it was opened. The delegate is now held per thread (each partition runs
  its whole step on one thread). Regression test `PartitionRangeItemReaderTest`.

### Changed
- **Batch jobs run in 2 streams.** Migration V5 sets `concurrency = 2` and `throttle_limit = 2` for every chunk job (was 4-8), and 2 is the fallback when
  a chunk job has no value. The number of streams stays a per-job setting (`batch_job_config.concurrency`, editable from the admin portal's
  Configuration screen or the batch admin API).
- **Batch service: Swagger UI** at `/swagger-ui.html` (OpenAPI JSON at `/v3/api-docs`) for the batch admin API, behind the same HTTP Basic login
  (`batch-admin` / `BATCH_ADMIN_PASSWORD`). Added `springdoc-openapi-starter-webmvc-ui` 2.6.0 and `OpenApiConfig`; `BATCH_SWAGGER_ENABLED` (default on,
  default off in the `prod` profile, set `true` in `render.yaml`) switches it.
- **Batch service: Kafka publishing can be switched off** (`BATCH_KAFKA_ENABLED=false` / `batch.kafka.enabled`). When off, no producer is created and
  `KafkaJobEventBridge` is not loaded, so nothing tries to reach a broker (this removes the endless `localhost:9092 ... could not be established`
  warnings on setups without Kafka). Default `false` in the `local` profile, `true` in `prod`; `render.yaml` sets it to `false`, docker-compose and the
  integration test keep it on.
- **E2E suite no longer stops on missing products or on one failing account.** The plan is fitted to the products that exist
  (`ProductResolver`, `e2e.products.fallback=AUTO|SKIP`): a missing/inactive/mortgage product is replaced by the closest existing active
  unsecured product or the account is skipped, and out-of-range amounts/tenures/rates are moved inside the product's limits. Product trouble is
  now a preflight warning instead of a blocking problem; only unreachable services, disabled jobs, a wrong business date or no usable product
  stop a run. An account that fails to be created or disbursed (`SETUP-01/02/03`) is recorded and the others carry on.

### Added
- **Maintenance: start from scratch.** Two new options in the admin portal (screen **Maintenance**): *Create base products* and *Clear all data*.
  - lending-service: `GET /api/v1/maintenance/status` (whether clearing is enabled, row counts, which base products exist),
    `POST /api/v1/maintenance/clear-data` (body `{"confirm":"RESET"}`) and `POST /api/v1/maintenance/base-products`.
    Clear empties loan accounts, schedules, accruals, EMI transactions, mortgages and valuations, the ledger outbox, the idempotency log and the
    loan product master in one `TRUNCATE ... RESTART IDENTITY` transaction, and keeps the business date, holidays, benchmark rates and the batch
    service's tables. It answers 403 unless `LENDING_RESET_ENABLED=true` (default false), 400 without the confirmation word, 409 while end of
    day is running. Base products are the eleven standard and sample ones from V10 and V11 (PL01, BL01, EL01, GL01, AL01, HL01 and the schedule-type samples TL01, BB01, AB01, SU01, EM01 that the end-to-end scenarios use); existing codes are left unchanged.
    Creating the base products is always allowed.
  - Admin portal: Maintenance screen with row counts, a typed-RESET confirmation, and the two actions; `/api/admin/maintenance/*` forwards to
    lending-service with the signed-in user as `X-Actor`. The portal still never writes to the database itself. `render.yaml` sets
    `LENDING_RESET_ENABLED=true` for the sandbox lending-service.
  - Not built with Maven yet (the frontend type-checks and builds).
- **Business date (task 1).** The platform now has one business date, owned by lending-service, and jobs run for it instead of the calendar date.
  - lending-service: migration V13 (`business_date_state`, `business_date_history`, `non_working_day`); `/api/v1/business-date`
    (`GET` state, `POST /eod/start` OPEN to EOD_RUNNING, `POST /advance` EOD_RUNNING to next day, idempotent on `expectedBusinessDate`,
    `PUT` to set the date forward while OPEN, `GET /history`, holiday calendar `GET|PUT|DELETE /holidays`, `GET /working-day`). State changes
    take a row lock. Weekends come from `business-date.weekend-days`. Seeded with today (India time).
  - Value dates are checked against it: EMI, part-payment and foreclosure dates, accrual, delinquency and rate-reset dates may not be after the
    business date (`business-date.enforce-no-future-dates`, default on). Statements are dated by it. Monthly partitions are created for the
    business date's month when the date advances or is set, and at startup.
  - Batch service: `BusinessDateProvider` (source `LENDING`; `SYSTEM` only for standalone use). `BatchJobContext` no longer falls back to the system
    clock. Cron and manual launches without a date use the business date. New end-of-day run (`EndOfDayService`): starts EOD, runs the enabled
    DAILY jobs in priority order for the business date, stops at the first job that does not complete, and only then advances the date; a re-run
    resumes without repeating finished jobs. Nightly at `batch.eod.cron` (default 01:00 India), and on demand with
    `POST /api/v1/batch-admin/eod/run` (202; poll `GET /api/v1/batch-admin/eod/status`). While `batch.eod.enabled=true` the DAILY jobs' own crons are ignored.
  - Admin portal (task 1c): days past due, overdue and upcoming lists, the dashboard, account figures and the accrual-summary default now use the
    business date, read from `business_date_state` over the existing read-only connection (`BusinessDateSource`; falls back to the wall clock with
    a logged warning if the table is missing). New `GET /api/admin/business-date`; the sidebar shows the date and whether end of day is running.
